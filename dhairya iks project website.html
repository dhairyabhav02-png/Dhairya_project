IKS research

Research, Vision & Objectives

Our Vision

To connect India's traditional Ayurvedic knowledge with modern technology and make credible information easier to access.

Our Mission

To develop a user-friendly digital platform that organizes traditional knowledge, presents reliable sources and demonstrates responsible AI.

Our Objectives

  1. Digitize selected traditional Ayurvedic information.
  2. Build an easy-to-use herbal knowledge library.
  3. Demonstrate AI-assisted educational answers.
  4. Provide references and distinguish traditional uses from scientific evidence.
  5. Evaluate the website with student feedback.
